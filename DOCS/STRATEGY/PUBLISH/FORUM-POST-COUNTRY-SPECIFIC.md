Sharing a free tool I built for Bangladeshi SMEs dealing with NBR compliance deadlines.

**What it does:**

- Unified calendar for all major NBR tax types: VAT (Mushak-9.1), Income Tax, TDS (Form 108), VDS, and Advance Tax
- Calculates late filing penalties automatically
- Exports a ready-to-import CSV for Manager.io Journal Entries — one click
- Shows how many days until each deadline with colour-coded urgency (red/amber/green)
- Bilingual: English and Bengali
- Mobile-first — works on any phone or desktop browser

**Live tool (no login, no signup):**
https://tools.inspiron.tech

**Source code (MIT license — fork it freely):**
https://github.com/INSPIRONTECH/COMMUNITY-TOOLS

---

**Why I built this:**

Bangladesh has 7+ million NBR-registered VAT businesses. Most small operators track deadlines on paper or in WhatsApp groups. There's no free, unified compliance calendar that covers all tax types in one view — especially one that exports directly into an accounting system.

The Manager.io CSV export feature means you can import accrued liability journal entries directly into your chart of accounts without manual data entry.
